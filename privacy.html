Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

LL Personal MCP is a personal tool used only by its owner, Lian Lim (lglim3@gmail.com), to access his own Google account data.

  • What it accesses: the owner's own Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar and Apps Script data, and only when the owner asks his AI assistant to.
  • Where data goes: data is processed on the owner's own computer. OAuth credentials are stored in the owner's macOS Keychain. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Other users: the app has no other users and collects no data about anyone else.
  • Revoking access: access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google user data is handled in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
